ProofCam Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

ProofCam stores your work records on your device. It has no account system. Optional AI uses a narrowly scoped ProofCam service only when you explicitly request a draft. This policy explains what leaves your device and why.

No account

ProofCam has no sign-up and no login. The optional AI service authenticates the app installation and, when applicable, verifies a signed purchase with Apple; it does not create a ProofCam user profile.

What stays on your device

Your photos, GPS coordinates, addresses, notes, logos, stamped images, and comparison records are stored in ProofCam's own storage on your iPhone. They stay there unless you explicitly request an AI draft, share or export selected content, or save it to Apple's Photos library. Device backups and Photos synchronization follow your Apple settings.

Camera

The camera is used to take the photos you stamp and compare. Capture, stamping, comparison layout, and manual notes are processed on your device. Camera photos are not sent for AI unless you open the AI disclosure, check availability, and choose Continue.

Optional AI descriptions

When you explicitly request an AI draft, ProofCam creates reduced clean-source JPEGs of the selected before and after photos, removes embedded metadata, and sends those two images plus the note you entered through ProofCam's Cloudflare-hosted service to OpenAI. Capture dates, stored GPS coordinates, addresses, project IDs, photo IDs, and stamped overlays are not added to the AI request. Anything visibly present in a photo or typed in your note is included. Manual notes and exports remain available without AI. Photos are processed in service memory and are not stored by ProofCam's application database. Result text and request identity are retained for up to 7 days for recovery; infrastructure backups may retain result text for up to 30 days. The OpenAI API request uses store:false and API data is not used for training by default, but OpenAI abuse-monitoring retention may apply, commonly for up to 30 days. ProofCam does not promise zero retention or a specific processing region. See OpenAI's API data controls and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Location

With your permission, ProofCam reads your location while the app is open to add GPS coordinates and an approximate address to a stamp. Online address lookups send coordinates to Apple's geocoder. If you enable the optional location map, Apple MapKit receives the map region needed to create the map thumbnail. Open in Maps passes the selected location to Apple Maps only when you tap it. These services follow Apple's privacy policy. You can use ProofCam without location in time-only mode.

Photo library

ProofCam adds a stamped photo to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos and grant add-only access. To add a company logo, you can select an image with Apple's photo picker; ProofCam receives only that selected image, not unrestricted access to your library.

Purchases

ProofCam Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple's In-App Purchase. To unlock and restore Pro, RevenueCat receives purchase information and a pseudonymous app-user identifier. The optional AI service also sends Apple's signed transaction proof to Apple for current-state verification and binds the resulting allowance to an authenticated app installation. These identifiers are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. See RevenueCat's and Apple's privacy policies.

Private analytics

ProofCam uses privacy-conscious analytics to understand app launches, onboarding, feature use, paywall results, purchases, restores, shares, and export reliability. Analytics events never include photos, GPS coordinates, addresses, note text, filenames, email addresses, or persistent photo IDs. TelemetryDeck receives an app-install identifier, event names, coarse event details, app and device metadata, and hourly-rounded timestamps for analytics only. This is not used to track you across other apps or websites. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

No ad tracking

ProofCam contains no advertising SDKs and does no cross-app or cross-site tracking, so it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Deleting your data

You can delete any stamped photo or comparison inside ProofCam. Deleting the app removes its local app-container data, but previously exported files, Photos copies, backups, the Keychain purchase identifier, and short-lived AI recovery text held by processors may remain for the retention periods described above. Apple, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, and OpenAI retain data under their applicable policies. For a privacy request, contact the address below.

Feedback and ratings

Feedback is sent only when you choose Send in the mail composer or your email app. Your message and email address are received by support. App and iOS versions are included only if you enable that option; photos, location, and purchase receipts are not attached automatically. Rating requests use Apple's system interface; ProofCam cannot see the rating you submit or whether Apple displayed the prompt.

Children

ProofCam is a general-purpose work tool and is not directed at children.
