ProofCam Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

ProofCam is built to keep your photos and the information on them on your device. There is no account and no ProofCam server. This policy explains what that means in plain terms.

No account, no ProofCam server

ProofCam has no sign-up and no login. We do not run a backend that receives your photos, locations, or notes. There is nothing for us to see.

What stays on your device

Your photos, GPS coordinates, addresses, notes, logos, and stamped images are stored in ProofCam's own storage on your iPhone. ProofCam does not upload these files to a ProofCam server. If you choose Share or Save to Photos, the selected content is passed to your chosen app or Apple's Photos library. Device backups and Photos synchronization follow your Apple settings.

Camera

The camera is used to take the photos you stamp. Those photos are processed on your device. ProofCam does not send them anywhere.

Location

With your permission, ProofCam reads your location while the app is open to add GPS coordinates and an approximate address to a stamp. Online address lookups send coordinates to Apple's geocoder. If you enable the optional location map, Apple MapKit receives the map region needed to create the map thumbnail. Open in Maps passes the selected location to Apple Maps only when you tap it. These services follow Apple's privacy policy. You can use ProofCam without location in time-only mode.

Photo library

ProofCam adds a stamped photo to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos and grant add-only access. To add a company logo, you can select an image with Apple's photo picker; ProofCam receives only that selected image, not unrestricted access to your library.

Purchases

ProofCam Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple's In-App Purchase. To unlock and restore Pro, our payments provider RevenueCat receives purchase information and a pseudonymous app-user identifier. This identifier links purchase activity within ProofCam; it is not used by ProofCam for advertising or tracking across other apps. It is stored in the iOS Keychain and can remain after reinstalling the app. See RevenueCat's and Apple's privacy policies for their purchase-data handling and retention.

Private analytics

If you enable Share private analytics in Privacy & About, ProofCam uses privacy-conscious analytics to understand app launches, onboarding, feature use, paywall results, purchases, restores, shares, and export reliability. Analytics events never include photos, GPS coordinates, addresses, note text, filenames, email addresses, or persistent photo IDs. TelemetryDeck receives a pseudonymous device-local identifier that can survive reinstalling, event names, coarse event details, app and device metadata, and hourly-rounded timestamps for analytics only. This is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Private analytics is off by default and can be turned off again at any time. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Optional usage and crash reports

Share private analytics is optional and off by default. If you enable it in Privacy & About, TelemetryDeck receives the coarse analytics described above and PostHog receives approved app events, event timestamps, app version, device metadata, a random paywall-presentation identifier and a pseudonymous identifier stored in Keychain that may survive reinstalling. RevenueCat receives that PostHog identifier to connect purchase events to app usage. Photos, exact location, addresses, notes and email are not included. Screen recording and automatic screen or text capture are disabled. Turning this option off stops new TelemetryDeck and PostHog app-usage events and requests removal of the PostHog identifier attribute from RevenueCat. This update depends on network delivery. Separately, RevenueCat may send purchase events to PostHog under its purchase identifier for revenue reporting; past records and links follow each provider's retention policy. Share crash reports is independently optional and off by default; when enabled, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash diagnostics, app/device information and its installation identifier. ProofCam adds no customer identifier or photo content to crash reports. Turning crash reports off disables automatic crash-report collection and deletes unsent reports. Contact support for requests concerning previously transmitted data.

No ad tracking

ProofCam contains no advertising SDKs and does no cross-app or cross-site tracking, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Deleting your data

You can delete any stamped photo inside ProofCam. Deleting the app removes its local app-container data, but previously exported files, Photos copies, backups, and the Keychain purchase identifier may remain. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records under their policies. For a privacy request, contact the address below.

Feedback and ratings

Feedback is sent only when you choose Send in the mail composer or your email app. Your message and email address are received by support. App and iOS versions are included only if you enable that option; photos, location, and purchase receipts are not attached automatically. Copy feedback writes your draft to the clipboard only when you tap it. Rating requests use Apple's system interface; ProofCam cannot see the rating you submit or whether Apple displayed the prompt.

Children

ProofCam is a general-purpose work tool and is not directed at children.
