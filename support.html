ProofCam Help & Support

Need a hand? Email canacar192@icloud.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Below are the questions we hear most.

Why does ProofCam ask for location?

Location lets ProofCam add GPS coordinates and an approximate address to your stamp. ProofCam only reads location while the app is open. You can decline and still use ProofCam in time-only mode — it stamps the date and time without a location. To change your choice later, open the Settings app › ProofCam › Location.

Why does ProofCam need the camera?

ProofCam is a camera, so it needs camera access to take the photos it stamps and compares. If you declined, open the Settings app › ProofCam › Camera and turn it on. Photos are processed on your device. They leave it only when you share or export them, or when you ask for an optional AI draft of a before/after comparison.

How do I save a stamped photo to my Photos library?

With Pro, open a stamped photo, open More actions, and tap Save to Photos. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos. This action does not need permission to read your library. Stamped photos are also kept inside ProofCam, so saving to Photos is optional.

I already bought ProofCam Pro. How do I restore it?

Open Settings › Privacy & About and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy or subscribe to ProofCam Pro, it will unlock again at no charge. This works for subscriptions and for one-time purchases made earlier.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose ProofCam Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does ProofCam do with my data?

ProofCam stores photos and their details on your iPhone, without an account. Online addresses and optional maps use Apple services. Your chosen exports go to the app or Photos you pick. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. If you ask for an AI draft, two reduced copies of the before and after photos and your note go through ProofCam's service to OpenAI. Usage analytics (TelemetryDeck, PostHog) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are sent only if you turn them on in Privacy & About. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I delete a stamped photo?

Open a photo and choose More actions > Delete, or use Gallery selection. Confirming removes it from ProofCam. Photos copies and other exports must be deleted separately. Removing the app removes its local container data; the Keychain purchase identifier and external copies may remain.

Why is there no address on some photos?

Addresses come from Apple's geocoder, which needs an internet connection. Offline, ProofCam still stamps the date, time, and GPS coordinates, and simply leaves the address off.

What does ProofCam Pro unlock?

Free includes one stamped photo, with the next free photo available 24 hours after your last successful capture, with a small ProofCam mark. You can view, inspect, and delete your photos inside ProofCam. ProofCam Pro is a weekly or yearly subscription that unlocks unlimited captures, Share, Save to Photos, PDF and branded multi-photo reports, removes the mark from new captures and their exports, and adds projects, logo, map, and job details.

How do AI drafts work?

In a before/after comparison, tap Draft with AI, check availability and choose Continue. ProofCam sends two reduced copies of the photos, without GPS, address or the stamp, plus your note, and returns a draft of the changes that are visible. Review and edit it before you share; AI can be wrong and cannot see hidden work. Free includes a few tries; subscriptions include a daily and monthly allowance shown in the app, and extra AI draft packs can be bought. Manual notes always work, also offline.
